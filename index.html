Security budgets need clearer answers.

What we do

From public signals to a clear plan.

BlackStar gives leadership, finance and security teams a shared, financial view of cyber risk, and a practical plan for where resources should go, both in advance and during an incident.

Why we built it

Decisions deserve better inputs

Security spending is often decided from lists of technical issues. We built BlackStar so those decisions can rest on likely impact and cost instead.

What it is

A decision platform

A web platform that assesses an organisation from public information, estimates its financial exposure and recommends how to allocate security budget.

What you receive

An ongoing service

Continuous monitoring, an exposure estimate in your currency, a prioritised plan, alerts when things change, and reports ready for your board.

Step by step

What happens, and how.

Scroll through the five stages. Each one shows what BlackStar is doing and, in plain terms, how it does it.

01 · Gather

We collect what the internet already knows.

What's happening: BlackStar builds a picture of the systems your organisation exposes publicly, such as websites, portals and email services.

How: it reads public records like website certificates, internet-scan listings and published vulnerability lists. It never connects to your systems.

02 · Anticipate

We think like the attackers.

What's happening: different attackers want different things. A ransomware group, a credential thief and a supplier-borne attacker each start in different places and head for different targets.

How: BlackStar models each type with its own entry points, targets and weight, and the weights can be adjusted for each organisation.

03 · Connect

We see how one problem could spread.

What's happening: systems rarely stand alone. A shared login or a supplier link can carry an incident from one system to the next.

How: BlackStar maps those links and follows every path an incident could take, to see which systems are most exposed.

04 · Weigh

We compare every way to spend the budget.

What's happening: each combination of protections is tested, including how an attacker would change course in response.

How: BlackStar compares the combinations that fit your budget and keeps the one that leaves attackers the least room, not simply the most severe issue first.

05 · Decide

You receive a clear, costed plan.

What's happening: the result is an expected yearly loss, a range for difficult years, and the steps to take first.

How: everything is expressed in your currency, with the source of every figure, ready to share with leadership. It updates as conditions change.

Trust

Figures you can rely on.

Every number BlackStar shows states where it came from, so it can be discussed and presented with confidence.

Non-intrusive by design

Insight without intrusion.

BlackStar never connects to your systems. It works only from public, third-party sources such as certificate records, internet-scan data and vulnerability feeds, and every assessment begins with confirmation that you are authorised to request it.

How we protect your data
  • Access keys stay on our servers and never reach your browser.
  • Every request is checked before any lookup runs.
  • We query trusted public sources only, never your infrastructure.
  • Each organisation's data is kept separate. We store no passwords.
  • If a source is unavailable, we show the last confirmed result and its date.
